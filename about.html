On Jeffrey Thompson

I grew up just outside Washington, DC and received my BFA from the Maryland Institute College of Art in Baltimore and have since been dwelling in a rowhouse with my drafting table and a cat. I taught courses on Style in Children’s Book Art for the University of San Diego and reviewed images for half a decade for Shutterstock.

I published my first book, The Midnight Ride of Paul Revere, for National Geographic and have worked steadily since its publication as a freelance illustrator.

My editorial work is usually done on scratchboard before being rendered digitally as vectors and I push for an expressionistic feel with these images. I also produce hand-pulled relief prints of my work.

I can be commisssioned but my rates depend on the project. Feel free to contact me at jef71o@gmail.com if you have a gig or a pitch you would like me to look at.
